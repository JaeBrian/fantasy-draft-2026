# Receiver diversity preference

When a recommended WR shares an NFL team with an owned WR, prefer an eligible WR from an offense with no owned WR when all three conditions hold:

- Recommendation score is within 6% of the original candidate (absolute magnitude for negative scores).
- Risk-adjusted projected production is at most 0.5 points per week lower.
- Probability of reaching the upcoming pick is at most 5 percentage points lower.

These are conservative policy limits chosen for the user's roster-diversity preference. They are not fitted risk estimates or evidence that avoiding teammates improves win probability. Scores, projections, ADP, opponent forecasts and player eligibility remain intact. A teammate with a clear value advantage can still rank first. The existing RB and TE rules continue to apply.

The recommendation card identifies the owned receiver and shared quarterback/offense exposure. Promoted alternatives explain the roster-diversity preference. The same advisor serves every seat, the production draft room and the isolated test room.

Limitations: this does not add a joint quarterback-injury or target-share model. Unknown off-board players have no stored NFL team, so they cannot contribute to this preference. Shared byes remain handled by the existing scorer and marginal-lineup evaluator. Earlier aggregate teammate correlations do not establish independence for each receiver pair.

- A saved-board advisor fixture prefers Wan'Dale Robinson over Matthew Golden while keeping Reed ahead on a clear value advantage.
- Full application test suite and production build passed.
- 300 complete 14-round skill drafts: 16,800 advisor decisions, 0 failures, 8.6 ms average per call.
- 36 complete 16-round drafts: 6,912 advisor decisions, 0 failures; every seat filled all ten starters.
- Browser checked the policy explanation and rendered the Watson/Reed warning using the actual AdvisorPanel with a controlled roster.

These draft checks validate functional behavior and roster completion. They do not establish a measured championship advantage. Saved morning study guides were not regenerated by this change; the previously documented Emily agreement-gate limitation remains.
